# Design Context

## Users

- General citizens creating, reviewing, sharing, and signing official documents.
- Institution staff collaborating on documents in their capacity as citizens and authorized representatives.
- Users may work quickly on phones or tablets, or spend longer focused sessions editing on laptops and desktops.

## Jobs To Be Done

- Find, organize, create, edit, share, verify, export, sign, and lock documents.
- Understand document status, access, save state, signing readiness, and the next permitted action.
- Review secure invitations and authenticity evidence without exposing protected document data.

## Brand Personality

- Trustworthy, calm, precise, and reassuring.
- Preserve the current light lavender and soft off-white workspace.
- Printable paper remains white and visually separate from application chrome.

## Design Principles

- Keep the document and its workflow state visually primary.
- Use restraint: lavender identifies trusted actions and focus, not decoration.
- Adapt controls for touch and narrow screens without changing document geometry.
- Keep high-risk actions explicit and evidence-led.
- Use scoped CSS Modules for component presentation and globals only for tokens, document-level defaults, shared editor geometry, and unavoidable global editor selectors.
